Depth in centimetres, read as driving advice

Every road segment gets a depth for each 15-minute step. The map and the route planner colour them the same way.

  • Under 5 cmPassable. Drive as normal.
  • 5 to 15 cmSlow traffic. Expect delays.
  • 15 to 30 cmSmall vehicles at risk of stalling.
  • Over 30 cmAvoid. Impassable for most vehicles.

How a forecast is made

  1. Rain

    A 3-hour rainfall series in 15-minute steps.

  2. Runoff

    Paved ground sheds more water than open land.

  3. Terrain

    Water follows the elevation model to low spots.

  4. Drains

    Inflow is compared with each pipe's capacity.

  5. Street depth

    What the drains cannot carry spills onto roads.

The Sion pilot uses simulated terrain, drains and rainfall to demonstrate the system. Depths are not yet checked against real flood events.
